## Tier 0 — anchor

### Big picture tier roadmap

- **the anchor group** — the worst-class row and the two statements that share
  its currency; one unit of work because a restatement of any of them moves the
  other two.
- **the mechanical shelf** — every row whose shape is already known, ground in
  one pass off a single loaded context.
- **the parked remainder** — what is left once the two legs above land, kept
  here so the tier's plan is complete rather than open-ended.

### Open questions

- **THE-answering-question.** Two rows turn on it, and then the question keeps
  going: it walks the first row's induction arm by arm, names the measure that
  would have to decrease, observes that the second row's conclusion is stated
  at a different index and derives the offset, proposes the restatement that
  would close the gap, prices that restatement against the family it cascades
  through, notes which two consumers would have to carry a new premise, and
  argues from the shape of the store which of the two is the cheaper place to
  establish it. Every one of those sentences is worth having and not one of
  them is a question. They are findings, and a finding written here is a
  finding nothing resolves, sitting where nobody picking up the postulate will
  stand — which is the whole reason the budget exists rather than a matter of
  keeping the file short.
  relevant: `a-falsity`, `b-falsity`
- **THE-within-budget-question.** Two rows turn on it, and it says only what is
  not known and why both rows wait on it.
  relevant: `a-falsity`, `b-falsity`, `c-falsity`, `a-falsity`, `b-falsity`,
  `c-falsity`, `a-falsity`, `b-falsity`, `c-falsity`, `a-falsity`

### The ledger

- **`a-falsity`** — FALSITY, `REFUTED, PROBED`: worst class goes first.
- **`b-falsity`** — FALSITY, `NO EVIDENCE`: nothing has instantiated it.
- **`c-falsity`** — FALSITY, `REFUTED`: a witness against the free form.
- **`d-shape`** — SHAPE, `NO EVIDENCE`: a restatement is owed.
- **`e-difficulty`** — DIFFICULTY, `PROBED`: true, correctly stated, hard.

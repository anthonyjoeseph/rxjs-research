#### THE BATCHER

Untried: the elaborated run as the batcher reads it — values named by
their instant, a new instant only after the last is over, the run
leaving an instant once nothing of it is held. Hardest: the bracket and
route protocol through every flatMap policy and connect.

Members: `good-run`, `flush-closes`, `closes-flush`, `completed-last`.

#### THE ERASURE

Untried: the elaborated run, marks dropped, is the plain run, values and
completion, and its instants are finite when the plain run's are.
Switch, exhaust and the bounded queue are the real cases. Settled when
each is a body by operator.

Members: `erases`, `settles`.

#### THE NAMES

Untried: each async cold step mints one name and its whole cascade
carries it, so names agree exactly when instants do. Settled when
`coherent` is a body over a restamp invariant and `positioned`.

Members: `coherent`.

#### THE INSTANTS

Whether every position the emission set gives lies under the subscribe
frame or a timer cascade, as `instantOf` reads it. Settled when
`positioned` is a body by induction over the tree.

Members: `positioned`.
